Meet Esma

Fifteen years of English teaching in Turkey and Australia

Hi, I'm Esma 🌿 I've been teaching English for over 15 years. I completed my bachelor's degree in English Language Teaching in Turkey, and my master's in education in Australia. Over the years I've had the chance to work with students of different ages, levels and cultures, both in Turkey and Australia.

For me, learning English is so much more than memorizing rules or exam techniques. In my lessons, I care about progressing according to the student's level, goals and learning style — about really being able to use English and communicate with confidence.

For IELTS & PTE preparation too, instead of applying the same program to everyone, I build a personalised roadmap by identifying each student's strengths and areas to develop. Because everyone's goal, way of learning and journey is different. 🌱

Your goal. Your pace. Your learning journey.

— Esma

How I teach

Learning shaped around you

Personalised to your goals

Every learner is different. I plan lessons around your level, goals and learning needs — whether you're preparing for IELTS or PTE, improving your everyday English, or building confidence in speaking.

English you can actually use

Language is more than grammar and vocabulary. My lessons focus on using English actively and naturally, helping you communicate with greater confidence in real-life situations.

Clear guidance and progress

We identify where you are, where you want to go and what is holding you back. Through focused practice and regular feedback, we work step by step towards measurable progress.

Engaging learning for children and young people

For younger learners, lessons are interactive, encouraging and age-appropriate. I use their interests, curiosity and strengths to help them speak English more naturally and develop a positive relationship with the language.
