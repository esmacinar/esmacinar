Meet Esma

Fifteen years of English teaching in Turkey and Australia

Hi, I'm Esma 🌿 I've been teaching English for over 15 years. I completed my bachelor's degree in English Language Teaching in Turkey, and my master's in education in Australia. Over the years I've had the chance to work with students of different ages, levels and cultures, both in Turkey and Australia.

For me, learning English is so much more than memorizing rules or exam techniques. In my lessons, I care about progressing according to the student's level, goals and learning style — about really being able to use English and communicate with confidence.

For IELTS & PTE preparation too, instead of applying the same program to everyone, I build a personalised roadmap by identifying each student's strengths and areas to develop. Because everyone's goal, way of learning and journey is different. 🌱

Your goal. Your pace. Your learning journey.

— Esma

How I teach

A warm, child-led way of learning

Play and inquiry come first

Children learn most when they're curious and enjoying themselves. I follow each child's interests and questions, so lessons feel like discovery rather than drills.

The child as a capable protagonist

Reggio-inspired, I treat every child as competent and creative. I notice what they can do, build on it, and let them take the lead in their own learning.

Connected to nature and real life

Drawing on my forest-kindergarten and permaculture training, I weave the real, tactile world into our sessions — bringing wonder and meaning to what we explore.

Inclusive, and shaped around your child

I adapt to each child's pace, needs and background — including children who need extra language or learning support — so every learner feels they belong.
